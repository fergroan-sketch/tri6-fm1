# TRI-6 FM-1 factory bank

All 128 patches were made for this firmware, around its own engine: three oscillators, sync, poly mod, the filter's feedback and drive, and the eight-slot mod matrix. Every patch after INIT gives the MORPH knob (ALGO, or CC 16) a job; it is listed after the name. Turn PRESET to step through them, or hold OCT- or OCT+ while turning it to jump ten.
