TRI-6 FM-1

a Trigon-6 style polysynth for the M-VAVE FM-1 · · play it in your browser

This is the real firmware, run unchanged on an emulated FM-1 by fm1-emu. Press Start in the emulator, then play with your computer keyboard or a MIDI keyboard. Chrome or Edge work best.

Open the player full size Install on your FM-1 Source on GitHub

Sound stuttering? The FM-1 runs at 240 MHz; the emulator starts lower to suit slower computers. Raise Clock under the emulator if your computer keeps up, or lower VOICES on TRI-6's GLIDE / PLAY page. The first start downloads the firmware (600 KB).

Playing it

On your keyboard
A … 'play C4 to F5 (black keys on the row above)
Z Xoctave down / up
SpacePLAY: chord memory (play a chord, then each key plays it)
Knobsdrag or scroll a knob on the panel; PRESETS / SELECT change the patch, ALGORITHM ten at a time
